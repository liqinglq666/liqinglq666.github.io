02 / ABOUT

Behind the work

A researcher who likes making things useful.

Research & Development

My interests span fiber-reinforced composites, microstructure–property relationships, and scientific computing. I turn recurring analysis tasks into reusable tools and explore connections between experiments, models, and material behavior.

AI & Research Automation

I design AI-enabled workflows around research problems, connecting data preparation, computational analysis, visualization, and technical documentation. My focus is on clear task definitions, reusable tools, and results that can be checked against physical models and experimental evidence.

Experimental Analysis

I use XRD, thermal analysis, LF-NMR, and DIC to study microstructure and mechanical behavior. My tools help process measurements, compare results, and make analysis easier to repeat.

Sharing What I Learn

I create practical tutorials with annotated screenshots and clear workflows. My tutorial collection covers XRD data processing and thermodynamic modeling.
